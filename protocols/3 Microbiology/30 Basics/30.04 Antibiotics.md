- **Carbenicilin:**
	- Blocks peptidoglycan linking, leading to **lysis** of new cells in 2-4h.
	- Resistant cells make β-lactamase that leaks outside and reduces the local antibiotic concentration, leading to **satelite colonies**.
	- Use **instead of ampicillin** which degrades quickly.
- **Chloramphenicol:**
	- Binds to ribosome, causing **imminent** (within minutes) **translation halt**.
	- **No lysis**, thus needs plating to see effects.
	- **Reversable** if antibiotic removed.
- **Kanamycin:**
	- Binds to ribosome, causing protein mistranslation and cell death
	- **No lysis**, thus need plating to see effects.
